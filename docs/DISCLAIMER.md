# Stonkwell disclaimer

_This is a draft for review by qualified counsel in each jurisdiction where Stonkwell is offered. It is not legal advice._

**What an Equity Token is.** Equity Tokens on Robinhood Chain are tokenized debt securities issued by Robinhood Assets (Jersey) Limited. Each one tracks the price of a listed company's stock or an exchange-traded fund. Holding an Equity Token, directly or through a Well, is **not** ownership of the underlying company or fund. It gives no voting rights, no right to attend meetings, and no direct claim on the company's assets. Any dividend or corporate-action adjustment is set by the issuer's terms, not by the company. You are exposed to the issuer's credit and operational risk as well as the price of the underlying stock.

**What a Well share is.** A Well share is a claim on the Equity Tokens and USDG held by that Well's smart contracts. It is **not** a stablecoin, a deposit or a savings product. It is **not principal-protected, not insured, and not guaranteed** by Stonkwell, Robinhood, the Equity Token issuer, the USDG issuer, Uniswap, Chainlink or anyone else. Share value can fall. That includes impermanent loss when the Equity Token price moves, swap costs on USDG exits, and losses from smart-contract failure.

**Yield Rate is not a forecast.** The Yield Rate shown for a Well is trailing swap-fee income over a recent window, annualized against the current Held Value. It is a historical figure that can change or fall to zero at any time. It does not account for price moves in the Equity Token and is not a promise of future returns.

**Credit Lines can be liquidated.** Borrowing on the Borrow Desk puts your pledged Well shares at risk. Collateral is valued with Chainlink prices, which pause outside market hours. When markets reopen, prices can gap past your liquidation threshold before you can act, and your collateral can be sold to liquidators at a discount.

**Market hours and corporate actions.** When a price feed is stale or the issuer flags a corporate action, sinking, USDG exits, rebalances, borrowing and liquidations stop. Only in-kind redemptions and repayments stay open during those periods.

**$WELL.** $WELL is a utility token with a fixed supply. It is not equity in any company and carries no right to profits, fees or governance unless one is explicitly established on-chain. The drawdown-and-retire mechanism reduces supply. It does not create a claim on protocol revenue and does not support any price.

That includes jurisdictions subject to comprehensive sanctions, and any others the Equity Token issuer excludes. By using the interface you confirm that you are not a restricted person and that using it is lawful where you are. The smart contracts are public and permissionless. Restricting the interface does not change your legal obligations.
